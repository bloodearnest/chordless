Chordless

Make a Google account with your own email address

Chordless signs you in with a Google account. You do not need a Gmail address: Hotmail, Outlook, Yahoo and any other address can be a Google account, and it takes about five minutes. For most invites any Google account will do, and a new Gmail address is the easiest one to make. Use these steps if you want to use the email address you already have, or if your invite is for an admin, which needs exactly the address it was sent to. Do this before you sign in.

If you already have a Google account with that address, skip this and open your invite.

  1. Open Google's sign-up page and enter your name. (If Google asks who the account is for, choose For my personal use.)

  2. Enter your date of birth and gender (you can choose "Rather not say").

  3. This is the step that trips people up. Google now offers you a new Gmail address. Do not choose one. Tap Use your existing email, the small blue link under them.

  4. Type the email address your invite was sent to, and tap Next.

  5. Google emails a six-digit code to that address. Open that mailbox (it may be in the junk folder), then type the code into Google.

  6. Choose a password. It is for your Google account, not for Chordless. Then follow Google's last pages to the end.

  7. Go back to your invite and open it. Tap Sign in with Google and use the account you just made.

If it does not work

  • If Google says the address is already used, it already has a Google account: sign in with it (or use "Forgot password"), then open your invite.
  • If Google will not take your address, tell whoever invited you. They can invite a Gmail address, or another Google account you can use, instead.
