Library repair

Merges songs that are the same song twice (a change of key is not a variant), and gives every song without a known source one: SongSelect where its chart has the CCLI material, otherwise the person who wrote it. Preview first; nothing changes until you apply. Merged songs go in the Drive trash at the next sync, so sync that space afterwards. Also available in the console as libraryRepair. Served by just serve only, never in production.
