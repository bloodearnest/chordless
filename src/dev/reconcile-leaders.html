Reconcile leaders

One-off. Setlists that other leaders led, but that say you lead them. Pick the emails those leaders sent (.eml files: docs/TESTING.md, "Real emails", has a script to download them); each setlist is matched to the email for its date (the songs are only compared to choose between emails for the same date), and the email's sender is proposed as its leader. Nothing is saved until you click Apply, and only the rows ticked. This changes the current organisation's setlists on this device; they reach Drive at the next sync.
